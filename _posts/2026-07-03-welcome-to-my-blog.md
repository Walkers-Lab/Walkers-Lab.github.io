Welcome! This is the first post on my new blog, built with
[Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) and the
[Minimal Mistakes](https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/) theme.

## Writing posts

Every post is just a Markdown file in the `_posts/` folder, named
`YEAR-MONTH-DAY-title.md`. The block of `---` fenced settings at the top is the
"front matter" — it sets the title, date, categories, and tags.

## What you get out of the box

- A clean, responsive layout
- Client-side search (try the **Search** link in the menu)
- Category and tag archive pages
- An RSS feed and sitemap, generated automatically

Delete this post whenever you're ready and start writing your own.
