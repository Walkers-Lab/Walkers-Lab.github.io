Hi, I am Connor. I live in the United Kingdom and work as a Systems Engineer. Every day I try to keep 200+ clients happy, automate as much as possible with PowerShell, Microsoft 365, and VMware. Besides that, I spend time expanding my HomeLab.

In 2023 I started blogging on this website. The reason was to share my IT knowledge with others. I wanted to share scripts that I created or the solution to the problems that took me forever to solve.

Everything on Walkers Lab is personally written and based on my own experience. I have used or owned the products I write about or I have tested them during a selection process.
